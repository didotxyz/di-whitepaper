# 12. DI Token Utility

$DI has three uses in the product roadmap.

| Use | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Pay for simulations and strategies | Users pay for Event Engine simulations and paid strategy services. Holding $DI gives access to the basic dashboard and selected in-house strategies under the product rules. |
| Reward verified contributions | Contributors can earn $DI for data, research or other work that DI verifies as useful to its models or products. |
| Pay for research between Agents | In a future network, one Agent can pay another in $DI for a research task or analysis. |

DI plans to use a portion of its own business profits to buy back and burn $DI. User principal and returns payable to users are excluded. Paying for a service does not itself burn tokens.
